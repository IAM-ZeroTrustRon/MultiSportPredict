## Travel and fatigue — the strongest factor

**Phoenix:**
- Played **Saturday 19 Sep in Perth**, beat the Wildcats 100–79.
- Now play **Monday 21 Sep in Hobart**. About 48 hours, a flight across the continent (normally routed through Melbourne), and two hours lost moving east.
- Tipster at Before You Bet: *"Backing any team on a Perth to Hobart double header is exceptionally risky, especially when their entire tactical identity relies on a high-energy pressing system."*
- Their Perth win was built on energy: they trailed 65–62 after three quarters, then won the 4th 35–17. Perth's coach said Phoenix "wore us down over 40 minutes." That fourth-quarter surge is exactly what a 48-hour turnaround takes away.

**Tasmania:**
- Home, rested, first regular-season game. No travel.
